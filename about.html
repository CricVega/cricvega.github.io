About CricVega

CricVega is a free, fast and lightweight website for following cricket. It is built for fans who want the score now — on any phone, even on a slow connection.

What you get

  • Live scores for international, league, domestic and women's cricket, refreshed automatically.
  • Ball-by-ball commentary with the current over, batters, bowler, run rates and target.
  • Full scorecards with fall of wickets, squads and playing XI, match info and points tables.
  • Schedules and results for the week, series pages, team pages and player profiles.
  • Cricket news from ESPNcricinfo and BBC Sport.
  • Six languages — English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, தமிழ், Español and Français — plus dark mode.
  • Installable as an app on Android, iPhone and desktop.

Where the data comes from

Match data is loaded directly in your browser from ESPN's publicly available cricket feed, which is powered by Cricinfo. News headlines link to the original publishers. CricVega is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with any cricket board, league or broadcaster.
